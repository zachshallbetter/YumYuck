The interaction I’d add that Tinder doesn’t have

I think there’s one interaction that could become the signature of YumYuck.

The “Lift”

Instead of swiping immediately, the user can pick the card up by holding it for about 300 milliseconds.

When they do:

* The card lifts off the background by roughly 16–20 pixels.
* The shadow deepens.
* The background subtly blurs.
* A soft haptic fires.
* Three invisible lanes appear around the card:
    * Left: Nope
    * Up: Maybe
    * Right: Yum

Now the interaction feels less like throwing a card away and more like placing it into one of three piles on a table. That physical metaphor aligns with the decision process: “Where does this belong?” rather than “Accept or reject?”

It also creates room for future gestures without adding UI:

* Down → “Already had this recently.”
* Long hold → “Why am I seeing this?”
* Double tap → “Tell me more.”
* Two-finger hold → Pin as a favorite.

That single change transforms the experience from a dating-app clone into something that feels purpose-built for decision making. The interaction itself becomes part of the product’s identity.
